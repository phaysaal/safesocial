Terms of use

Version 1, 29 October 2026. You agree to these when you create an identity in the app.

Spheres is a private social network: everything you share goes only to the people in the sphere you chose, encrypted on your phone. That privacy is for ordinary life, not for harming people. These terms say what is not allowed, how to report it, and what we do.

Who we are

Spheres is made and run by Counterself (in formation). Contact: safety@spheres.dev.

What is not allowed

  • Anything that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including child sexual abuse material of any kind, grooming, and sexual messages to a minor. We have zero tolerance for this; see our child safety standards.
  • Threats of violence, incitement to violence, and content that promotes terrorism.
  • Harassment, bullying, stalking, and sharing intimate images of someone without their consent.
  • Hate directed at people for who they are.
  • Encouraging someone to harm themselves.
  • Scams, fraud, spam, malware, and impersonating someone else.
  • Anything else illegal where you or the people you share with live.

Who can use Spheres

You must be at least 13, or older where the law of your country sets a higher age for using services like this one without a parent's consent.

Reporting

In the app, Report to Spheres is on every post, every message from someone else, and every contact, and under Settings as Report a concern to Spheres. A report goes to the people who run Spheres, sealed on your phone so that only they can read it. You choose whether it includes the words and pictures you are reporting; without them we see only your reason and your note. Reporting to a sphere's admin, which is a separate thing, stays inside the sphere.

What we do

Because everything is encrypted end to end, we cannot read your spheres, and we do not scan them. We act on what is reported to us. We can:

  • stop an identity being found or added through our relay, so nobody new can reach it;
  • report child sexual abuse material, and threats to life, to the authorities as the law requires;
  • keep a report for as long as the law requires, and otherwise delete it once dealt with.

We cannot remove something from the phones it was sent to, and a conversation already set up between two phones does not pass through anything we can single out. Admins of a sphere can remove posts and members of their sphere; you can block anyone.

Your content

What you post is yours. You give the people you share it with the right to see it in the app, and nothing more. We never sell it, show it to advertisers, or train anything on it.

No warranty

Spheres is a pre-alpha, provided as it is. It has not been independently audited. We are not liable for losses caused by using it, as far as the law allows.

Changes
