Child safety standards

Last changed 29 October 2026.

Spheres has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These are our published standards.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that depicts, promotes or normalises the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a child to abuse or exploit them.
  • Sexual messages, images or requests sent to anyone under 18.
  • Sextortion, and any attempt to buy, sell or trade such material.

These rules are part of our terms of use, which every user agrees to before they can post.

How to report

In the Spheres app: Report to Spheres on the post, the message or the contact, and choose A child is at risk, or child sexual abuse material; or Settings, Report a concern to Spheres. The report reaches us directly, sealed so that only we can read it. You may also write to safety@spheres.dev.

If a child is in immediate danger, call the police first.

How we handle it

  • Every child safety report is reviewed as a priority.
  • We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and national law enforcement, as the law requires.
  • We stop the reported identity being found or added through our relay.
  • We keep what was reported for as long as the law requires for the authorities, and no longer.

Spheres is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read spheres or messages and do not scan them; we act on what users report to us, and a report can include the reported content only because the reporting user chooses to include it.

Compliance

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including the reporting obligations of the United States (18 U.S.C. 2258A) and the European Union.

Contact for child safety

Our designated child safety contact can be reached at safety@spheres.dev. Law enforcement requests go to the same address.
